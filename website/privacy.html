Privacy Policy

ElevenVoice is built to keep your data on your own computer. This page explains what the website and the app do with information about you.

The app

ElevenVoice runs locally. Your recordings, cloned voices, scripts, videos and generated audio stay on your computer and are never uploaded by the app. There is no account and no sign-in.

The app connects to the internet only to:

  • download voice and speech models from Hugging Face or their publishers;
  • check GitHub for new versions;
  • use an online translator, only if you add your own API key, in which case the text you translate is sent to that provider.

Usage analytics are switched off in this build. If they are ever enabled, they will be opt-in and announced in the app and in this policy.

This website

The website does not use forms, accounts or advertising. It does collect the following:

  • Hosting logs. The site is hosted by Vercel, which processes your IP address and request details to deliver and secure the site.
  • Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights. These record page views and loading performance in aggregate. They use no cookies and do not identify you personally.
  • Country for language. On your first visit the site reads your country from Vercel's network, without storing it, to show the page in your language when your device is set to English.
  • GitHub. Your browser asks GitHub for the star count and the latest release, and downloads come from GitHub. GitHub sees your IP address.
  • Google Fonts and Google Translate. Fonts load from Google. If the page is translated, its text is sent to Google Translate. Google sees your IP address.

Your choices and rights

You can switch the page back to English from the language menu, which stops the translation. You can clear the site's storage in your browser at any time. See the Cookie Policy for what is stored.

Depending on where you live (for example under the LGPD in Brazil or the GDPR in the EU), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to processing. Because this project keeps no personal database, most requests concern the third-party services above. Contact them, or ask on GitHub Issues and we will help.

Children

The website and app are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page when anything changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
