Cookie Policy

The website uses very little browser storage, and none of it is used for advertising or tracking you across sites.

Third-party cookies

When the page is translated, Google Translate may set its own cookies, covered by Google's cookie policy. Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights use no cookies.

Managing storage

Choose English in the language menu to stop the translation and remove the googtrans cookie. To remove everything, clear this site's data in your browser settings. The site works normally without any of it.
